THE LEARNING JOURNEY

One clear step
at a time.

A guided route from understanding the workplace to practising and reviewing your learning.

  1. 01. Explore & learn

    Move around the 360° scene. Select its hotspots to open short lessons tied to workplace objects.

  2. 02. Pass the lesson checks

    Answer each popup check correctly. A wrong response keeps the hotspot incomplete so you can review and retry.

  3. 03. Practise & assess

    Complete 15 questions and topic activities. Five scenario sets vary between runs, with shuffled questions and choices.

  4. 04. Review your progress

    View saved attempts, scores and completion. Pass every mandatory topic at 70% or above to earn the Safety Passport.

BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

A few useful
answers.

Is this website the training application?

This is Team Galaxy’s promotional website. The training application opens separately using the “Open training platform” links.

Can trainees register themselves?

No. Accounts and training access are provided by administrators. There is no public trainee registration.

How are accounts protected?

The prototype uses role-based permissions, protected sessions and server-side answer validation. Optional authenticator verification adds a second sign-in step.

Is the Safety Passport an accredited qualification?

No. This academic prototype demonstrates digital learning and completion records. It does not replace an employer’s approved training or establish professional accreditation.

Does it require a VR headset?

No. The current system uses interactive browser-based 3D scenes. A headset-based VR version is a future possibility, not an implemented feature.
