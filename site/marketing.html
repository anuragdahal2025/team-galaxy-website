WHO WE WANT TO REACH

Useful conversations.

We focus on understanding and relevance rather than claiming a large audience. Our prototype is designed around practical warehouse learning needs.

WAREHOUSE & LOGISTICS MANAGERS

A clear view of who has assigned learning, what has been completed and where support is needed.

SAFETY & TRAINING COORDINATORS

Practical learning.

Workplace scenarios, guided lessons and activities that help explain everyday decisions.

SUPERVISORS & TRAINEES

THE NEXT FOUR WEEKS · PLANNED

to useful feedback.

The following are future targets. We will record evidence as activity happens rather than present plans as completed results.

  1. WEEK 1

    Share appropriately.

    Prepare an approved launch message and share the website through suitable university or professional channels.

  2. WEEK 2

    Show the journey.

  3. WEEK 3

HOW WE WILL EVALUATE IT

Measure what
actually happened.

Our marketing log will record the date, channel, material shared, evidence, response and follow-up. A published post proves publication; a response proves engagement.
